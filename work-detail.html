Henri & Hélène

Info

Henri & Hélène is a neighborhood French bistro with a warm, unhurried personality. We built its identity from hand-drawn illustration and a soft butter-yellow palette, then carried it across menus, coasters, packaging and signage. The goal was a brand that feels like an evening spent with friends, whether guests meet it on a clipboard menu or on the shopfront. Up+Above led the strategy, the visual identity and the print design from the first workshop to opening night.
